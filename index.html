How we build

Small on purpose.

A studio can be one idea done carefully. Here's what we hold to.

i.

Your device, your data

No servers, no cloud, no accounts. What you write is stored only on your phone — we never see it.

ii.

No tracking, ever

We don't collect analytics on you, sell data, or run ads. There's nothing to sell because we keep nothing.

iii.

Made to last

Buy it once, keep it. Our apps work offline and don't depend on us staying online forever.

iv.

More on the way

Readiary and Burn After Writing are the start. We're building quietly toward the next few.
