Meet BookLeaf

BookLeaf

BookLeaf is a reading journal app that helps you save books, write reading notes, and build your personal reading history.

📚 Reading Journal

Keep track of books you are reading, finished, or want to read.

✍️ Personal Notes

Write your thoughts, reflections, and favorite moments from each book.

🔒 Local First

Your reading records are stored on your device. BookLeaf is designed with privacy in mind.
