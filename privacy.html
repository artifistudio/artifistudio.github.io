Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

BookLeaf

BookLeaf is a reading journal app created by ArtiFi Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how BookLeaf handles user information.

Information We Collect

BookLeaf does not collect personal information from users. Your reading records, book entries, and notes are stored locally on your device.

Local Data Storage

BookLeaf is designed as a local-first app. The data you create in the app remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

In-App Purchases

BookLeaf may offer an optional premium upgrade through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment processing is handled by the respective app store provider. ArtiFi Studio does not directly receive or store your payment card information.

Analytics and Advertising

BookLeaf currently does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Children's Privacy

BookLeaf does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at artifistudio.help@gmail.com.
